EDITCOREAI — carpeta app-extracted completa
================================================

1. Cierra EDITCOREAI.
2. D:\PROGRAMAS IA\EDITCOREAI\resources\
3. Renombra app-extracted → app-extracted.backup
4. Descomprime este ZIP.
5. Renombra la carpeta interior a: app-extracted
6. Colócala en resources\app-extracted
7. Regenera asar:
   cd "D:\PROGRAMAS IA\EDITCOREAI\resources"
   Rename-Item app.asar app.asar.old -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
   npx --yes @electron/asar pack app-extracted app.asar
8. abre EDITCOREAI.exe

Nombre del producto: EDITCOREAI (sin EditFlow).
